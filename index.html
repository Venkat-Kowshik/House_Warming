Welcome

The muggu invites prosperity

Drawn at the threshold of a new home, the muggu is a prayer in colour. It welcomes Goddess Lakshmi and every good thing that is meant to arrive with her.

Blessing

The toran guards the doorway

Fresh marigolds and leaves hung at the entrance greet every guest and keep the home open only to good energy. A doorway tied with flowers is a doorway ready for blessings.
